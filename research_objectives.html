Research Objectives

  • Ball Handling Skills Analyzer Create a system that assesses a player's ball handling skills, including hand-eye coordination, ball control, and passing accuracy.
  • Attacking Skills Analyzer Establish a system that evaluates offensive skills including precise shooting, effective mobility, and quick decision-making.
  • Defensive Skills Analyzer Create a tool that assesses defensive abilities including footwork, positioning, and pass-interception capabilitiess.
  • Injury Prevention and Classification By detecting tiredness-related problems, integrate biological data (such as heart rate and exhaustion) to track player health and avoid injuries.
  • Team Selection Optimization To improve team chemistry and strategy, use data-driven insights to assist coaches in choosing the top-performing players for particular scenarios.
