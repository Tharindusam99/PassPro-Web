Research Gap

Pose estimation's limited use in netball performance analysis is one significant gap. Netball's quick and complex motions necessitate a more sophisticated method, even though posture estimation techniques are well-established in sports like basketball, where player movements are more linear. The adaptation of posture estimation algorithms to evaluate netball-specific motions, such pivoting, dynamic passing, and quick direction changes, has received minimal attention.

There is a substantial research gap in the application of sports analytics technology to netball, despite the expanding corpus of work in this area, especially in the areas of pose estimation and machine learning in performance evaluation. Sports like basketball, soccer, and tennis, where machine learning and posture estimation have previously been effectively combined, have been the subject of most of the work to date. Netball, which poses difficulties because of its quick tempo and extremely dynamic character, has not, however, seen a thorough investigation of these technologies. ways.

The absence of real-time feedback in conventional netball performance analysis is another significant flaw. Existing approaches depend on subjective training observations or post-match evaluations, which may result in lost chances for quick progress. During practices or games, the use of real-time data from technologies like posture estimation can provide useful information that allows for quick modifications. Although other sports have effectively used this real-time strategy, netball has not embraced it widely.
